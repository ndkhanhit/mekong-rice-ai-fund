2. Executive Summary (Limit: < 75 words)
72 / 75 words ✓

Mekong Rice AI integrates our working cooperative platform (MGA Coop360) with FarmUAV aerial diagnostics and simple Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) water monitoring for smallholder rice cooperatives in Vietnam’s Mekong Delta. Over 12 months, we will field-validate this prototype across 40 hectares (paired AWD vs. control plots) to prove 20–30% irrigation water savings, 100% pesticide Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI) safety compliance, preserved yields, higher farmer net profits, and verifiable low-emission rice traceability.

3A. Domain 1 — Sustainable & Regenerative Practices (Limit: < 100 words)
86 / 100 words ✓

Continuously flooded Mekong Delta rice paddies waste irrigation water and generate major agricultural methane (CH4) emissions alongside excess agrochemical runoff. Mekong Rice AI combines low-cost AWD field tubes, sentinel water-level sensors, hyperlocal weather alerts inside MGA Coop360, and FarmUAV crop-stress imaging to guide timely drainage without yield loss. In paired treatment-versus-control cooperative plots, this prototype targets 20–30% less irrigation water, 10–15% lower fertilizer and pesticide inputs, reduced field losses through early pest/water-stress detection, and 30–40% estimated CH4 reductions tracked transparently via IPCC Tier 2 water-regime protocols.

3B. Domain 2 — Safe & Nutritious Food for a Healthy Diet (Limit: < 100 words)
91 / 100 words ✓

Rice provides the primary daily calories for over 95 million Vietnamese and hundreds of millions across Southeast Asia, making chemical safety in staple rice critical to public health. MGA Coop360’s built-in Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI) Alert Engine automatically tracks every pesticide application logged in digital field diaries and blocks harvest lot certification with red/yellow/green safety alerts until safe withdrawal periods pass. Combined with moisture/quality grading (KCS) and FarmUAV precision spraying that cuts unnecessary chemical use, the system delivers residue-safe, clean staple rice backed by tamper-evident QR lot traceability from field to consumer.

3C. Domain 3 — Equitable Access, Affordability, Appeal & Trust (Limit: < 100 words)
83 / 100 words ✓

Smallholder farmers rarely adopt sustainable practices when tools are costly or buyers distrust paper records. Our cooperative-shared model spreads digital platform and UAV costs across member households (~USD 10–15/ha/season), reducing pumping and input costs so household net profit rises 12–20% without increasing retail rice prices. Consumers and domestic rice millers scan a lot-level QR code generated by Coop360 to verify safe pesticide withdrawal dates, water-saving practices, and harvest quality—making sustainably produced, residue-safe rice both economically affordable and visibly trustworthy for Global South households.

3D. Preventing Negative Trade-offs Across Domains (Limit: < 100 words)
90 / 100 words ✓

Poorly managed water saving can trigger weeds, moisture stress, or yield drops that hurt farmer incomes and food affordability. Mekong Rice AI mitigates this by applying "Safe AWD"—keeping fields flooded during sensitive germination and flowering stages—paired with FarmUAV early stress detection and a matched continuous-flooding control group inside the same cooperative. If yield or net profit lags control plots, irrigation thresholds are adjusted immediately. Meanwhile, our automated PHI alerts prevent farmers from compensating for weeds or pests with unsafe late-season pesticide applications, protecting both food safety and ecosystem health.

4. Stage of Development & Prototype Evidence (Limit: < 100 words)
89 / 100 words ✓

We are beyond concept stage with a working full-stack software prototype (MGA Coop360) built on Next.js, Spring Boot, and PostgreSQL, featuring 11 operational modules: farmer/plot GPS registry, seasonal plans, mobile offline-capable worklogs, input inventory, automated PHI pesticide alerts, harvest lot numbering, KCS quality grading, QR traceability, live Open-Meteo 7-day weather forecasts, and drone service booking (UC-11). This Seed Grant funds field integration of rice-specific AWD water-level telemetry, FarmUAV agronomic stress mapping, and our first controlled, side-by-side smallholder cooperative trial measuring water volume, yield, net profit, and counterfactual GHG reductions.

5. Degree of Innovation — What Makes This Breakthrough (Limit: < 100 words)
89 / 100 words ✓

Existing rice agtech solutions fail smallholders because they fragment into expensive standalone IoT hardware, unverified paper carbon audits, or enterprise tools inaccessible to cooperatives. Mekong Rice AI is novel in unifying cooperative daily ERP operations, automated PHI food-safety enforcement, shared FarmUAV diagnostics, and controlled AWD water-profit-GHG measurement inside one low-cost, mobile-first cooperative workflow. Instead of selling farmers an abstract "carbon app," we embed climate-smart irrigation and residue safety into the exact tool cooperatives use to manage inputs, dispatch drones, and prove household profit—unlocking voluntary adoption and trustworthy data simultaneously.

Part B — Technical Dossier, 12-Month Workplan, Budget & Pitch Video

10. Experimental Field Validation Design (40–50 ha, 1 Full Comparative Season)

To maximize scientific rigor within a lean $25,000 Seed Grant, the field trial uses a paired-plot controlled design inside one committed Mekong Delta rice cooperative:

  • Treatment Plots (CONFIRM: 25–30 ha; ~20–25 households): Full Mekong Rice AI package — Coop360 mobile logging + Safe AWD tubes & sentinel water sensors + weather-linked irrigation alerts + automated PHI pesticide safety checks + 3–4 scheduled FarmUAV flights.
  • Control Plots (CONFIRM: 15–20 ha; ~10–15 households): Same rice variety, planting week, soil type, and dike compartment (ô bao), following Business-as-Usual continuous flooding, tracked with identical pump-energy logs, input receipts, and crop-cut yield sampling.
  • Four Measured Outcomes: (1) Irrigation Water Volume (m³/ha & pumping cost), (2) Paddy Yield (t/ha at 14% moisture via crop-cuts + weighbridge/KCS), (3) Household Net Profit (digital receipt-verified), and (4) Controlled IPCC Tier 2 CH4 Estimate + 100% PHI Safety Audit + Consumer/Buyer QR Trust Check.
