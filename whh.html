2. Executive Summary (Limit: < 75 words)
72 / 75 words ✓

Mekong Rice AI integrates our working cooperative platform (MGA Coop360) with FarmUAV aerial diagnostics and Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) water monitoring for rice cooperatives in An Giang, Vietnam’s Mekong Delta. Over 12 months, we will validate this prototype across 45 hectares (30 ha AWD vs. 15 ha control) to prove 20–30% water savings, 100% pesticide Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI) safety compliance, preserved yields, higher net profits, and verifiable QR rice traceability.

3A. Domain 1 — Sustainable & Regenerative Practices (Limit: < 100 words)
87 / 100 words ✓

Continuously flooded Mekong Delta rice paddies waste irrigation water and generate major agricultural methane (CH4) emissions alongside excess agrochemical runoff. Mekong Rice AI combines low-cost AWD field tubes, sentinel water-level sensors, hyperlocal weather alerts inside MGA Coop360, and FarmUAV crop-stress imaging to guide timely drainage without yield loss. In paired treatment-versus-control plots in An Giang, this prototype targets 20–30% less irrigation water, 10–15% lower fertilizer and pesticide inputs, reduced field losses through early pest/water-stress detection, and 30–40% estimated CH4 reductions tracked transparently via IPCC Tier 2 water-regime protocols.

3B. Domain 2 — Safe & Nutritious Food for a Healthy Diet (Limit: < 100 words)
91 / 100 words ✓

Rice provides the primary daily calories for over 95 million Vietnamese and hundreds of millions across Southeast Asia, making chemical safety in staple rice critical to public health. MGA Coop360’s built-in Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI) Alert Engine automatically tracks every pesticide application logged in digital field diaries and blocks harvest lot certification with red/yellow/green safety alerts until safe withdrawal periods pass. Combined with moisture/quality grading (KCS) and FarmUAV precision spraying that cuts unnecessary chemical use, the system delivers residue-safe, clean staple rice backed by tamper-evident QR lot traceability from field to consumer.

3C. Domain 3 — Equitable Access, Affordability, Appeal & Trust (Limit: < 100 words)
83 / 100 words ✓

Smallholder farmers rarely adopt sustainable practices when tools are costly or buyers distrust paper records. Our cooperative-shared model spreads digital platform and UAV costs across member households (~USD 14/ha/season), reducing pumping and input costs so household net profit rises 12–20% without increasing retail rice prices. Consumers and domestic rice millers scan a lot-level QR code generated by Coop360 to verify safe pesticide withdrawal dates, water-saving practices, and harvest quality—making sustainably produced, residue-safe rice both economically affordable and visibly trustworthy for Global South households.

3D. Preventing Negative Trade-offs Across Domains (Limit: < 100 words)
91 / 100 words ✓

Poorly managed water saving can trigger weeds, moisture stress, or yield drops that hurt farmer incomes and food affordability. Mekong Rice AI mitigates this by applying "Safe AWD"—keeping fields flooded during sensitive germination and flowering stages—paired with FarmUAV early stress detection and a matched continuous-flooding control group inside the same cooperative dike compartment. If yield or net profit lags control plots, irrigation thresholds are adjusted immediately. Meanwhile, automated PHI alerts prevent farmers from compensating for weeds or pests with unsafe late-season pesticide sprays, protecting both food safety and ecosystem health.

4. Stage of Development & Prototype Evidence (Limit: < 100 words)
92 / 100 words ✓

We are beyond concept stage with a working full-stack software prototype (MGA Coop360) built on Next.js, Spring Boot, and PostgreSQL, featuring 11 operational modules: farmer/plot GPS registry, seasonal plans, offline-capable mobile worklogs, input inventory, automated PHI pesticide alerts, harvest lot numbering, KCS quality grading, QR traceability, live Open-Meteo 7-day weather forecasts, and drone service booking (UC-11). This Seed Grant funds field integration of rice-specific AWD water-level telemetry, FarmUAV agronomic stress mapping, and our first controlled 45-hectare smallholder cooperative trial in An Giang measuring water volume, yield, net profit, and counterfactual GHG reductions.

5. Degree of Innovation — What Makes This Breakthrough (Limit: < 100 words)
89 / 100 words ✓

Existing rice agtech solutions fail smallholders because they fragment into expensive standalone IoT hardware, unverified paper carbon audits, or enterprise tools inaccessible to cooperatives. Mekong Rice AI is novel in unifying cooperative daily ERP operations, automated PHI food-safety enforcement, shared FarmUAV diagnostics, and controlled AWD water-profit-GHG measurement inside one low-cost, mobile-first cooperative workflow. Instead of selling farmers an abstract "carbon app," we embed climate-smart irrigation and residue safety into the exact tool cooperatives use to manage inputs, dispatch drones, and prove household profit—unlocking voluntary adoption and trustworthy data simultaneously.
