2. Executive Summary

Vietnam’s Mekong River Delta produces over 50% of the nation’s rice and 90% of its rice exports, with An Giang Province alone contributing more than 4 million tons of paddy annually. Yet conventional continuously flooded rice cultivation generates nearly half of Vietnam’s agricultural methane (CH4) emissions while exposing smallholder farmers to rising diesel/electricity pumping costs, agrochemical overuse, and thinning net margins. Although Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) is scientifically proven to reduce irrigation water use by 20–30% and methane emissions by 30–48%, cooperative-scale adoption remains stalled by three structural bottlenecks: (1) farmers fear yield penalties without reliable field-level water guidance; (2) cooperatives lack affordable operational software to coordinate collective pumping, input tracking, and precision drone services across fragmented plots; and (3) buyers, millers, and climate finance actors lack credible, low-cost plot-level verification data.

Mekong Rice AI, led by An Giang-based impact enterprise Mekong Green AI Technology Co., Ltd., closes this last-mile gap by deploying an integrated cooperative digital stack—combining MGA Coop360 (our working mobile-first cooperative ERP with 11 live operational modules) with FarmUAV (aerial crop-stress/water-distribution mapping and shared drone service dispatch). Over 18 months covering two consecutive Mekong Delta seasons (Winter–Spring and Summer–Autumn), we will run a rigorous 150-hectare controlled cooperative pilot (100 ha AWD Treatment vs. 50 ha Continuous-Flooding Control across 120 smallholder households) in An Giang Province. Anchored on three transparent farmer-first metrics—irrigation water volume, standardized paddy yield, and household net profit—paired with a controlled counterfactual IPCC 2019 Tier 2 + static gas-chamber GHG protocol, PAA’s S$220,000 (~USD 165,000) catalytic grant will prove an economically self-sustaining cooperative service model (~USD 14/ha/season) ready to scale across Vietnam’s 1 Million Hectares Low-Emission Rice Program and Southeast Asian rice deltas.

3. Problem Statement & Systemic Bottleneck in Southeast Asia

  1. Water Inefficiency & Rising Pumping Costs: Traditional continuous flooding consumes 1,200–1,500 m³ of water per ton of paddy (7,500–9,500 m³/ha/season). Dry-season water stress and volatile diesel/electricity prices push cooperative pumping bills to 1.2–2.0 million VND/ha/season (~USD 48–80/ha) while degrading canal ecosystems.
  2. High Agricultural Methane & Input Overuse: Permanent anaerobic flooding emits an estimated 4.5–6.5 tCO2e/ha/season (especially with incorporated straw), while risk-averse smallholders overspend on nitrogen fertilizer (110–130 kg N/ha) and preventive pesticide sprays (5–7 sprays/season)—compressing net margins.
  3. The Cooperative Execution & Verification Bottleneck: Under Decision 1490/QD-TTg, Vietnam aims to convert 1 million hectares of Mekong Delta rice to low-emission production by 2030. On the ground, execution lags because cooperatives rely on retroactive paper logs, farmers lack subsurface water visibility to trust AWD drainage, and millers lack cheap plot-level verification to pay premiums.

Why PAA Catalytic Capital Is Decisive: Commercial investors require multi-season causal proof of yield protection and cooperative fee collection before financing agtech scale-up. PAA’s catalytic grant removes this market-entry bottleneck by funding two-season controlled validation, Coop360+FarmUAV rice-AWD integration, and commercial testing of a self-funding cooperative fee and QR-traceability offtake link.

4.3 Rigorous Counterfactual GHG Measurement Protocol (Zero Greenwashing)

  • Paired Experimental Design in One Closed-Dike Ô Bao: 100 ha Treatment (~80 HHs applying Safe AWD + straw baling/composting) vs. 50 ha Control (~40 HHs under BAU continuous flooding) — identical alluvial soil, rice variety (e.g., OM18 / Dai Thom 8), sowing week, and weather.
  • Primary Quantification (IPCC 2019 Tier 2 / IRRI SECTOR-aligned): Daily water-table depth from 18 sentinel loggers + geotagged timestamped AWD tube photos in Coop360 drives the emission factor equation:
EFi = EFc × SFw × SFp × SFo
EFc = Mekong Delta baseline flooded factor (kg CH4/ha/day); SFw = verified multiple-drainage factor; SFp = pre-season flooding factor; SFo = organic/straw management factor.
  • Empirical Static Gas-Chamber Calibration: Subcontracted scientists (AGU / CTU / CLRRI) perform closed-chamber gas chromatography across 8 paired Treatment/Control subplots during drainage and reflooding transitions to calibrate local parameters and report ±95% confidence intervals.
  • Tamper-Evident Audit Trail: Every drainage cycle requires dual verification (IoT trace or geotagged tube photo in Coop360 + FarmUAV surface-water orthomosaic check), fully aligned with Vietnam Decision 1490/QD-TTg MRV standards.
